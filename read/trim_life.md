# Returns matter over emotions*

People chase respect, courtesy, relationships but what if I tell you that, they all are for those who can serve someone the purpose they want. In this world nothing is free, everybody is here for himself/herself. You get respect when you serve someone the purpose they want. Dale Carnegie says in chapter 3rd of his book "How to Win Friends and Influence People in Society" that he got 300% increase in his hall's rent down to 50%, by talking tobthe manager of that hotel in the following balanced way:

1. Opening converstaion warmly
2. Mentioning the issue and immediately praising the manager.
3. Showing advantages and disadvantages along what he wants.
4. Ending the converstaion warmly

If you really want somebody to like you, do your work, be friendly or do some favour then **stop criticising** and **start praising**.

If you can't give what others want then they too can't give you something. Remember humans and other animals work on one principle **You get favour for what you can give back.**

Also you don't have to mention everywhere about what they get back in return, especially not in realtionships. I call this **Silent mutual exchange** while I will call the manager example **Open mutual exchange**.

Humans by evolution are profit seeking the way as other animals. This is a skill, an instinct that one must learn in the path of becoming mentally mature.
