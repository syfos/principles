# Returns matter over emotions*

People chase respect, courtesy, relationships but what if I tell you that, they all are for those who can serve someone the purpose others want. In this world nothing is free, everybody is here for himself/herself. You get respect when you serve someone the purpose they want. Dale Carnegie in chapter three of his book "How to Win Friends and Influence People in Society" tells how he got 300% increase in his hall's rent down to 50%. 

Here is the balanced approach in a nutshell: 

1. Open the converstaion with warmth.
2. Tell abiut what profit you can give.
3. Tell your problem.
4. Demand something clearly.
5. End conversation warmly.

Here is my example about how would I had handeled the same:

"
Dear Manager, 

Thanks for informing about the price surge by 300%. I understand your duties as the manager of this hotel and I am not here for blaming you because you are just doing your duty. My concern is about the price increase as neither me nor my clients who will be coming to the seminars are able to pay those bills. I have the tickets printed and sold but this price surge is driving me and my clients away to some other affordable hotels. Wellz let's take a look on:

Advantage the hotel will gets on 300% price increase for me:

1. Hall free to be rented to high biders.
2. More opportunity of acctracting customers through advertisemnents.

Now, the disadvantage this hotels can get from 50% price surge for me:

1. Hall will be booked for the entire year or more.

2. The seminars would attract cultured and civilized clients to your hotel, they likely will pay for food and stay.

3. The seminar to seminar volume for the course of the whole year may create a sense in the society that this hotel is good.

I desire only a 50% increase for me. Thanks for giving your time to my words.

Yours sincerly,
Pulkit
"

If you really want somebody to like you, do your work, be friendly or do some favour then **stop criticising** and **start praising**.

If you can't give what others want then they too can't give you something. Remember humans and other animals work on one principle **You get favour for what you can give back.**

You don't have to mention everywhere about what they get back in return, especially not in realtionships. I call this **Silent mutual exchange**. Whhile I will call the manager example **Open mutual exchange**.

Calculations about what you get back in return matters mostly for the professional life. For personal life it is better to read the intentions without saying what they want or you want. Though you must present what you want when it is important.

Dale Carnegie, in chapter 1 and 2 explains about **critisim and appreciation**. In chapter 1, he says one must avoid criticizing if they want the work to be done. In second chapter, Cargegie explains how **appreciation/praise** generates the warm and the friendly relation with one. Many parents fail at parenting not because of stress, but, becuase of criticizing there kids while lacking the praise the kid wants. 

Conclusion: **One may not care about you, if you fail at returning what they want**

In any **Open Mutual Converstaion** first tell what you are offering to them and then go for explaning things and after that demand what you want.
